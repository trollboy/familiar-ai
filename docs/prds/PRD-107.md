# PRD-107: Cheap-First Routing Is Live

## Objective

The owner's statement of the 1.0 routing behaviour, verbatim: *"Use the
local Ollama model if you can get away with it, then use Claude for the
heavy lifting, Codex for tests, or whichever you have the best luck
with."*

Every mechanism that sentence needs exists in the tree and none of it is
wired into a behaviour:

| Piece | Where | State |
|---|---|---|
| Per-stage routing through the worker registry | PRD-031, PRD-044 | live, but every attempt on this host ran `claude-code` |
| Escalate once to a stronger worker on verification failure | PRD-041 | built; `escalated_from_sequence` has zero rows |
| Probation and promotion on recorded outcomes | PRD-032, `run.rs` `worker_probation_policy` | built; archived with its status still reading "Ready for implementation" |
| A failed check becomes work for an implementer | PRD-096 | landed 2026-09-19 |
| A local worker reachable without a vendor CLI | PRD-094, PRD-100 | queued |
| A cost basis every worker can be ordered by | PRD-086 | queued |

The last two are why this PRD depends on them. Without a local rung there
is nothing cheap to try first; without a cost basis there is nothing to
order by. This PRD is the one that turns the pieces into the sentence.

## What the sentence means, precisely

- **"If you can get away with it"** is an escalation ladder, not a
  guess. The cheap rung tries; deterministic verification and the
  independent review decide whether it got away with it; if not, the
  next rung gets the same PRD plus the evidence of what failed. Once.
  The north star names retry-looping as the second fire, so the ladder
  has exactly one escalation and then stops at the ceiling or at a human.
- **"Claude for the heavy lifting"** is the declared risk tier as a
  floor. A PRD declaring `security` does not start on the local rung.
- **"Codex for tests"** is a job class. A failed required check, which
  PRD-096 now turns into a remediation request with the captured
  failure, routes to the worker declared for that class. Splitting a PRD
  into separate implementation and test-authoring streams inside one
  worktree is a different and larger design and is out of scope here;
  the owner's second reading is served, the first is named for later.
- **"Whichever you have the best luck with"** is PRD-032's probation,
  live and fed by PRD-086's cost rows and the review outcomes PRD-093
  tracks. Promotion is a recorded event with the numbers that caused it.

## Why the fifth criterion is a live run

Five PRDs in this corpus have stated an end-to-end criterion, PRD-017,
066, 077, 100 and 101, and every one was satisfied by a fake-agent
regression. The ledger on this repository records 30 attempts, 2
completions and 5 merge-queue commits, none of them unattended. This is
the PRD whose result the owner most wants as a number, so its acceptance
is the number: a row in `driver_attempts` on this repository, with the
ladder recorded. A fake-agent regression proves the mechanism and is
still required by the other criteria; it does not satisfy this one.

## Scope

The per-job-class ladder in registry configuration; ladder selection in
worker resolution; single bounded escalation carrying evidence, recorded
on the attempt; the unprofitable-ladder skip; probation live for the local
rung; report lines for ladder and cost per accepted PRD; the
provider-setup guide's ladder section; regressions in a dedicated test
file; and the live run.

Out of scope: any change to what PRD-086 measures; cross-vendor review
independence rules, which PRD-014 and PRD-079 own and this PRD obeys;
splitting implementation and test authoring into separate streams;
retiring or deprecating either vendor CLI.

## Assumptions (recorded at specification time, 2026-09-22)

- PRD-041's escalation seam generalizes from "verification failed" to
  "the cheap rung's attempt was not accepted" without a second mechanism.
  If it does not, the finding is reported before a second one is built.
- A local worker's cost basis is orderable against hosted workers through
  the policy PRD-086's eighth criterion requires. If that policy is
  absent, the ladder reports the local rung as unrankable and starts one
  rung up rather than guessing.
- The owner's baseline from PRD-098, cost per accepted PRD for five people
  on Claude, is the number the fifth criterion's report is compared to
  once declared. Its absence does not block this PRD; it blocks the claim
  that this PRD made anything cheaper.
