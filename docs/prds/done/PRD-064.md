# PRD-064: Typed Resource Reservations

## Objective

One provider-neutral, resource-typed reservation contract for every
finite capacity Familiar schedules against — money, tokens, and
hardware alike — with an atomic, attributable, crash-safe lifecycle
that lets warrants compose with parallelism instead of disabling it.

## Problem

The reservation mechanism was specified inside PRD-053, welded to
provider-cost reconciliation and therefore transitively to the
Anthropic organization collector (PRD-052) — while PRD-063 needs the
identical lifecycle for accelerator memory and inference slots, and
PRD-056's scheduler needs it for global ceilings. Local hardware
scheduling must not require administrative billing. Meanwhile ledger
B1 remains open: any finite cost/token warrant silently forces
`component_parallelism` to one because nothing can attribute spend
per component.

## Design

**Typed resources.** A reservation names a resource type and amount
from a closed, extensible vocabulary: `nanousd-budget`,
`uncached-tokens`, `total-tokens` (where policy permits raw-token
ceilings), `accelerator-memory`, `system-memory`, `inference-slots`,
`model-loading-slots`, `exclusive-runtime`, and further typed finite
capacities as later PRDs declare them. Amounts are integers in the
resource's canonical unit; unknown capacity is a property of the pool
(policy decides refuse-or-serialize; PRD-063), never of the ledger.

**Lifecycle.** `acquire` is atomic against the pool's remaining
capacity; multi-resource requests are all-or-nothing unless the
caller explicitly accepts partial grant. Held reservations `commit`
(settle against observed consumption at durable stage boundaries),
`release` (return unused capacity), `expire` (deadline passes without
settlement), or are `recovered`. Recovery is governed by a
provider-neutral **owner-liveness contract**: every reservation is
held by a `ReservationOwnerIdentity` — a globally unique owner
instance carrying installation identity where applicable, a random
nonce or generation, an owner kind, and its
project/execution/component attribution — and a liveness provider
supplies durable, provenance-recorded evidence resolving that exact
instance to live, provably dead, or ambiguous. Only *provably dead*
releases; ambiguous owners retain their reservations or follow an
explicit conservative recovery policy, never releasing as though
dead (PRD-039 discipline). This PRD defines the contract and its
acceptance without requiring any particular liveness provider;
consumers supply their own evidence. Renewal exists only
where a resource type justifies it and is an explicit, attributed
act. Every transition is durable before any dependent acknowledgement.

**Attribution and honesty.** Each reservation records its owner
identity and project/execution/component attribution, so per-component
warrant spend is a fact, not an estimate — the B1 fix: components
draw independent reservations from the session warrant and
parallelism composes with finite ceilings; an unaffordable
reservation refuses that component loudly. Settlement against
observed consumption records overrun explicitly when actuals exceed
the commitment; unknown observed consumption settles conservatively
per declared policy (hold the reservation, or settle at the reserved
amount with an unknown-consumption mark) and never converts to zero.
Contention resolves deterministically (stable ordering over requester
identity and arrival), reproducible from persisted state.

**Denominations.** Monetary reservations are canonical nanoUSD
(PRD-051); token reservations distinguish uncached from raw totals so
a ceiling can permit cache-heavy work and still stop runaways — the
mechanism half of ledger B8, whose data half (distinct categories,
price schedules) is PRD-051's.

**Consumers.** PRD-053 consumes this contract for monetary warrants
and adds reconciliation semantics; PRD-056's scheduler consumes it
for global and per-project ceilings and supplies daemon/worker
owner-liveness evidence (its process-start identity, nonce, claim
generation, and orphan-recovery states) into the owner-liveness
contract; PRD-063 consumes it for hardware capacity. None of them
re-implements the lifecycle, and none is required for this PRD's
acceptance.

## Ledger defect ownership

Owns B1. Owns the mechanism half of B8 jointly with PRD-051's data
half. B5, B6, and B13 remain execution/recovery work as before.

## Scope

- Reservation types, pools, persistence, lifecycle, and repository
  methods; per-component drive-loop integration replacing the
  parallelism kill-switch.
- Uncached-token and nanoUSD warrant denominations wired into
  existing budget enforcement (PRD-024 semantics preserved).
- Deterministic tests per the acceptance criteria.
