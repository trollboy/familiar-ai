# PRD-063: Local Inference Runtime and Resource-Aware Scheduling

## Objective

Make local models real Familiar workers: a local-runtime adapter
contract under the PRD-058 loop, artifact-verified worker identities
over the PRD-062 registry, honest resource telemetry with no invented
dollars, and daemon scheduling that treats developer hardware —
Apple Silicon first — as the scarce reserved resource it is.

## Design

**Worker identity.** A local worker is a full PRD-057 spec:
`provider = local`, a `RuntimeId` (`unsloth`, `ollama`, `llama-cpp`,
`mlx`, `vllm`, or another explicitly configured backend), a PRD-062
`ModelArtifactId`, a capability profile, an endpoint or process
profile, and a hardware/resource profile where relevant. The same
artifact under MLX and llama.cpp is two workers; empirical history
never crosses that line.

**First runtime (verified 2026-08-30; re-verify at implementation).**
The smallest useful path matches both the codebase and the owner's
Mac: **externally managed OpenAI-compatible local endpoints**,
covering Unsloth's serving mode (`unsloth start` exposes an
OpenAI-compatible API; Unsloth supports macOS natively per its
documentation) and Ollama (already probed by PRD-047 and spoken by
the existing `familiar-ai-llm` client). One neutral local transport,
two distinct RuntimeIds with their own capability profiles —
OpenAI-compatibility is transport reuse only, never a claim of OpenAI
behavior (PRD-061's discipline applies locally too). MLX-native,
llama.cpp-direct, and vLLM backends are explicitly backlogged behind
the same contract; process-managed lifecycles (Familiar launching and
supervising the server) arrive in a later phase. Nothing assumes
CUDA-specific behavior on Apple Silicon.

**Runtime contract.** Adapters implement, behind PRD-058: health and
capability probes (non-executing where possible; a minimal inference
probe only where the operator accepts it — local tokens are cheap but
not authority-free); model load/unload and cold-start reporting;
streaming; tool-call formats (native where the backend has one,
schema-constrained templates otherwise, reliability scored
empirically per PRD-058); structured output; context limits;
cancellation; concurrency and queue depth; memory-pressure behavior;
crash and endpoint-disappearance detection; usage reporting; optional
local authentication by external reference. **Artifact
verification:** the adapter verifies the endpoint's claimed model
against the registered artifact wherever the runtime exposes digests
or metadata; where it cannot, the worker runs as degraded
unverified-artifact and policy decides whether that routes.

**Telemetry, not invoices.** Local inference has no provider invoice
and none is fabricated. Typed, timestamped PRD-051 observations carry
token categories as reported (cached and reasoning included when the
backend exposes them; unknown stays unknown), wall time, time to
first token, tokens per second, load time, peak memory, accelerator
and CPU utilization, retries, and failures — attributed to the full
spec identity, project, execution, and stage like any other worker.
Energy is recorded only when credibly measurable. Operator-configured
allocation policies (electricity, amortized hardware, hosted
local-server cost) may later produce **declared estimates** with
policy identity, under the PRD-051 subscription-declaration
discipline: visible, versioned, never implicit, never provider
billing. Acceptance-rate and remediation metrics come from the
ordinary PRD-032 evidence, not from the adapter.

**Scheduling.** Hardware is the local rate limit. The PRD-056
scheduler acquires PRD-053-lifecycle reservations over accelerator
memory, system memory, inference slots, model-loading slots,
exclusive-runtime constraints, and thermal/power ceilings where the
platform exposes them; deadlines bound queued work. Capacity comes
from probed hardware/resource profiles; unknown capacity follows
explicit policy (refuse, or serialize conservatively) — never
optimistic invention. Two workers that cannot coexist are never
co-launched; reservation crash recovery releases dead holders.

**Security.** Loopback, LAN, and remote endpoints are distinct trust
classes; non-loopback endpoints may require TLS and authentication,
always by external reference (BYO-Auth). Artifact integrity comes
from PRD-062 digests. Weights and templates are untrusted data: a
malicious chat or tool-call template can shape text, but tool
authority, sandboxing, filesystem access, and environment come only
from PRD-058's rules — owned hardware grants nothing extra. License
metadata travels with worker displays. Telemetry is privacy-clean:
no prompts, no outputs, no credentials.

## Phasing

1. External-endpoint runtimes (`unsloth`, `ollama`) over the neutral
   local transport: probes, streaming, telemetry, artifact
   verification, degraded paths.
2. Hardware/resource profiles and reservation-aware scheduling in the
   daemon.
3. Process-managed lifecycle (launch, supervise, load/unload) for a
   verified backend; MLX-native or llama.cpp-direct per
   implementation-time verification of Apple Silicon support.
4. Operator allocation policies and vLLM/other backends as demand
   proves out.

## Assumptions (recorded 2026-08-30)

- Unsloth's macOS support and OpenAI-compatible serving come from its
  current documentation; its exact tool-call format, usage fields,
  and artifact-metadata exposure are implementation-time
  verification items, and its capability profile starts conservative.
- Ollama exposes model metadata sufficient for at least
  degraded-to-verified artifact mapping; where digests are
  unavailable, the degraded path is the honest default.
- Thermal and power telemetry availability varies by platform; absent
  sensors mean absent constraints, recorded as unknown rather than
  assumed safe.
