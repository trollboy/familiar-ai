# PRD-053: Cost Reconciliation and Attributed Reporting

## Objective

Close the loop between what Familiar estimated and what the provider
actually billed: deterministic reconciliation of PRD-051 local
estimates against PRD-052 authoritative rows, monetary warrants that
consume the PRD-064 reservation contract, and source-centric
reporting integrated into the PRD-055 series surface that labels
every amount's authority, coverage, and freshness.

## Design

**Reconciliation is new facts, not corrections.** A reconciliation run
reads a completed collection window and the locally attributed
estimates inside it, and appends reconciliation rows: matched amounts,
exact nanoUSD variance, and a status from a closed set — reconciled,
reconciled-with-variance, pending, mismatch, unattributed-provider-spend.
Raw provider rows and original estimates are never touched.
Workspace-attributed provider cost reconciles against locally
attributed executions where the dimensions allow; account-level cost
with no Familiar attribution remains visible unattributed spend — the
operator's other usage is not an error. Local executions unmatched
after the settlement horizon (default three daily buckets past window
end, configurable) become explicit mismatches. Variance within the
tolerance (default one cent — 10,000,000 nanoUSD — per source-window,
configurable, exact value always recorded) classifies as
reconciled-with-variance. Unexplained variance is reported, never
distributed. Re-running a window's reconciliation is idempotent, and a
superseding provider revision (PRD-052's revision model — corrections,
credits, restated reports) re-opens the affected window by appending
new reconciliation rows against the current-effective projection; the
earlier reconciliation rows remain as history.

**Warrants stay local and conservative.** Authoritative cost arrives
days late; it evaluates accounting accuracy, never live enforcement. A
live monetary warrant is enforced against locally attributable
estimates and PRD-064 reservations — the atomic per-component
lifecycle, uncached-token and nanoUSD denominations, and the B1 fix
all live in PRD-064; this PRD consumes them and adds the
reconciliation-side rules: settled reservations are evidence for
matching, and a later authoritative figure that exceeds the live
estimate is an accounting finding, never a retroactive warrant
violation. An execution with unknown usage keeps failing closed: it
cannot pass a finite warrant by recording nothing.

**Reporting answers the money questions.** Integrating into the
PRD-055 series and query surface (which already extends the PRD-035
stewardship surfaces and `usage`/`report` commands) — never a second
query implementation — callers can ask: what did this execution, session,
or PRD cost, and is that estimated or authoritative? What is
month-to-date per billing source, and in aggregate with attribution
intact? What share is unattributed or external? How much was cache
read/write versus uncached input/output? Which sources are stale,
incomplete, unsupported, or unauthorized, and what is the
reconciliation variance? Every response carries provenance and
freshness sufficient to keep an estimate from ever reading as an
invoice, and subscription declarations remain availability, never
spend. Read paths are cached-only; nothing refreshes over the network
unless its contract says so.

## Scope

- Reconciliation rows, matching policy, settlement horizon and
  tolerance configuration, and the deterministic reconciliation run
  (invoked by `billing collect` after a completed window and by an
  explicit `billing reconcile`).
- Monetary warrant consumption through the PRD-064 reservation
  contract; no reservation lifecycle is implemented here.
- Reconciliation and variance dimensions integrated into the PRD-055
  query surface; `usage` and the morning report label estimated,
  authoritative, reconciled, incomplete, and unknown amounts
  distinctly.
- Score inputs for PRD-032: cost per accepted PRD by worker with
  authority and completeness labels.

## Ledger defect ownership

B1 and the mechanism half of B8 moved to PRD-064 (B8's data half stays
PRD-051's); this PRD owns no ledger defect directly. Enables but does not fix B5
(honest stop reasons are run.rs logic), B6 (review-result
checkpointing), and B13 (finalize retry and the running-row sweeper);
PRD-051's stage-boundary observations bound B13's evidence loss.

## Assumptions (recorded at specification time, 2026-08-30)

- Daily provider buckets cannot attribute cost to individual
  executions; attribution below the workspace-day grain remains
  local-estimate authority, and reconciliation compares aggregates at
  the finest shared grain.
- The settlement horizon and tolerance defaults above are operator
  policy, not provider guarantees, and are expected to be tuned from
  observed reporting delay.
