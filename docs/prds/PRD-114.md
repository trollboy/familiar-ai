# PRD-114: Finished attempts give their worktree back

## Objective

By 2026-09-27 the worktree directory held 156 GB across seven attempts
whose PRDs were all archived, each carrying its own cargo target. The pool
filled, and PRD-107's Docker preflight failed with "No space left on
device" inside the container, a disk failure wearing a code failure's
clothes (FAM-BUG-113). Nothing in the daemon removes a worktree once its
attempt is over; `worktree.rs` only re-labels ownership sidecars on
recovery.

## Scope

- In `drive.rs`, at the point an attempt's outcome is recorded, decide
  whether the worktree is reclaimable: terminal outcome and
  `git rev-list --count <base>..<branch>` equal to zero.
- In `worktree.rs`, a `WorktreeLease::reap` that runs
  `git worktree remove --force <path>` from the repository, leaves the
  branch alone, and updates the sidecar's state to `reaped`.
- Removal errors are logged and swallowed; the outcome row is already
  written before reaping is attempted.

## Out of scope

- Age-based reaping of worktrees with unlanded commits (an operator
  decision, per `PRD Archiving Is The Owners Call`).
- Deleting branches or sidecars.
- The Claude-session worktrees under `.claude/worktrees`, which are not
  Familiar's.

## Evidence

`docs/running_bugs.md`, FAM-BUG-113; `du -sh
~/.local/state/familiar-ai/worktrees/*/*` on 2026-09-27 before cleanup.
