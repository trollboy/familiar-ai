# PRD-091: PRD Admission Quality and Reviewer Calibration

## Objective

Three of the four things Familiar cannot do share one root. It does not
decide what to build; it cannot survive a bad PRD; and it cannot
guarantee its reviewer is right. PRD quality is the input that determines
everything downstream and it is entirely human, while the gate that is
supposed to catch a bad PRD is itself a human gate — so a
confidently-built-wrong-thing is one tired approval away. On the other
end, the adversarial reviewer is a model: it caught a real HIGH defect
recently, and it will also miss things and invent things, and nothing
records which.

Neither end can be automated away, and this PRD does not try. It makes
both ends measurable: cheap machine checks that refuse an obviously bad
PRD before a model call, and outcome tracking that turns "the reviewer is
a model" from a caveat into a number.

There is also a live gap worth closing here: the structured PRD contract
validates a `status` field of `draft`, `ready`, `in_progress`,
`completed`, or `blocked`, but eligibility is computed from reconciled
backlog state and never consults it. A PRD marked `draft` sitting in the
active directory is selectable today. A quality gate whose own draft
marker does nothing is not a gate.

## Design

- **Refuse early, cheaply, and by name.** Testable-criterion shape,
  plausible expected files, dependency existence and acyclicity, risk
  vocabulary membership. Every one of these is decidable without a model
  and every failure names the field. The expensive failure mode is a
  model confidently building from a criterion nobody could verify.
- **Criteria and scope must agree.** A criterion that no declared file
  could satisfy is the specific shape of a PRD that will be built wrong,
  and it is detectable at admission.
- **Input quality is reported next to output quality.** The same surface
  that shows how the machine did shows how the specification did.
- **Calibrate the reviewer.** Findings resolve to confirmed, waived, or
  invalid with a reason. A per-reviewer summary makes review quality a
  measured input for PRD-032's probation instead of an assumption.
- **Measure, do not steer.** No route changes here, and no quality check
  can grant an approval — only withhold admission from work that has not
  earned it.
- **Honour the draft marker.** A status the contract validates must
  affect what runs.

## Scope

Admission-time quality checks and their durable results; the
criterion-to-scope agreement check; per-PRD quality reporting; reviewer
finding outcome tracking and the calibration summary; the probation
routing input; frontmatter status honoured by eligibility; regressions in
a dedicated test file.

Out of scope: writing or prioritising PRDs, which stays human; changing
any route or probation decision (PRD-032); review tiering (PRD-045) and
batch-tier review (PRD-071); and any attempt to judge whether a PRD is a
good idea, as opposed to whether it is a well-formed and internally
consistent specification.

## Assumptions (recorded at specification time, 2026-09-04)

- Testable-criterion shape is checkable heuristically with a low false-
  refusal rate; where a heuristic is uncertain the check reports rather
  than refuses, and the refusing checks are limited to the structural
  ones that cannot be wrong.
- Finding outcomes are attributable — a landed fix, a recorded waiver, or
  an explicit invalid judgement — for enough findings to calibrate; the
  unresolved count is reported so a thin sample is visible rather than
  presented as a clean score.
