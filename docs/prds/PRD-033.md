# PRD-033: Repository Delivery Policy and Approval Modes

**Status:** Ready for implementation
**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Depends on:** PRD-013, PRD-018, PRD-026, PRD-039

## Objective

Make publication, merge, and deployment a repository-owned policy with explicit
assurance and approval modes rather than project-specific shell assumptions.

## Scope

- Require each repository to choose delivery disabled, reviewed-PR/manual,
  PoC self-approval, or review-gated automatic delivery.
- Default to reviewed PR and stop before merge/deploy when automatic authority
  is absent.
- Permit PoC self-approval only under an explicit bounded warrant and visible
  low-assurance label.
- Turn `HumanReviewRequired` into an actionable decision surface instead of a
  terminal CLI error: attached commands render the findings and prompt the
  operator to retry remediation, accept the reviewed risk, or preserve the
  checkpoint.
- Permit unattended risk acceptance only in explicit PoC self-approval mode;
  bound it by PRD count and duration, prohibit production delivery, and retain
  the exact findings and stop reasons being overridden.
- Model review-gated approvals suitable for later separation-of-duties and SoC
  evidence without claiming certification.
- Configure provider adapters, environments, checks, migration gates, health
  checks, rollback, and least-privileged credential references.
- Persist every authority decision and external side effect idempotently.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Missing delivery policy cannot merge or deploy.
2. Manual mode creates or updates a reviewed PR and stops.
3. PoC self-approval is explicit, finite, non-production, and reported.
4. Review-gated mode proves implementer, reviewer, and approver constraints.
5. Partial publish/merge/deploy failures recover without duplicate side effects.
6. No repository, cloud, username, host, or privileged path is compiled in.
7. An attached `run` or `resume` that reaches `HumanReviewRequired` prints the
   findings and offers retry, accept-risk, and preserve choices; EOF/non-TTY
   preserves the checkpoint rather than guessing.
8. `drive` can continue past `HumanReviewRequired` only under an explicit,
   finite PoC self-approval warrant. The override, actor, findings, stop reasons,
   warrant consumption, and low-assurance label are persisted and reported.
9. Governed and manual modes never silently convert failed review into approval.
